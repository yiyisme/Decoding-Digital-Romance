RESEARCH DESIGN

研究框架与方法 Research Framework & Methods

研究结合 Xavier 文本语料的远读与细读，以及 109 份有效问卷。问卷通过多元回归考察五项玩家体验与情感依恋（Emotional Attachment，D3）的独立关联，并对 66 份实质性开放回答进行主题编码。Q5 最多允许选择两项沉浸体验，因此相关组间比较用于描述关联，不作因果解释。

A multiple regression tested five aspects of player experience at the same time. Longer bars indicate stronger independent associations with emotional attachment (D3); highlighted bars are statistically significant in the full model.

KEY FINDING

Technological / embodied immersion and daily virtual companionship showed the strongest independent positive associations with reported emotional attachment.

02

FEATURE SALIENCE

Does attachment differ depending on which features players found especially immersive?

Respondents could select up to two features that made them feel most immersed or emotionally close to a favourite character. Each line compares the average attachment score of respondents who selected a feature with those who did not.

A SURPRISING PATTERN

Story salience and relational attachment are not the same thing.

Respondents who selected Story reported lower average attachment than those who did not, while Interaction and Quality Time showed the opposite pattern. This does not mean that story reduces attachment: Q5 allowed up to two selections, so “Selected” indicates feature salience rather than an exclusive player type. These comparisons are associative, not causal.

03

PLAYER VOICES, AGGREGATED

What did players themselves say made the relationship meaningful?

Sixty-six respondents provided substantive open-ended answers. Rather than publishing individual responses, this public artefact presents only aggregated thematic patterns derived from the coding.

开放回答主题编码

WHAT PLAYERS FOREGROUNDED

The most frequently coded themes concerned real-life compensation, listening and validation, and the integration of the character into everyday routines. Counts include both primary and secondary theme assignments and are therefore not mutually exclusive respondent categories.

✦

FROM IMMERSION TO ATTACHMENT

Repeated presence matters.

The survey does not reduce attachment to one mechanic. Embodied design can make a character feel immediately present; narrative gives that character history and meaning; repeated communication allows that meaning to become part of everyday routine.

研究发现

文本分析与问卷结果共同呈现出多层次的情感设计：具身／技术沉浸与日常虚拟陪伴，与情感依恋的独立关联最强；粉丝身份也有关联，但程度较弱。故事依然是情感意义的重要来源，不过，选择“故事”作为最有沉浸感的功能，并未像反复互动功能那样与更高依恋相联系。被故事打动，与把角色体验为持续在场的关系对象，是彼此相关但不同的体验。

学术贡献

本研究提出“反复关系在场”（Recurrent Relational Presence），用于描述角色如何通过多次相遇，持续显得可感知、能回应，并逐渐进入玩家的日常经验。研究也将拟社会关系（Parasocial Relationship）与实时服务型游戏中的互动设计联系起来：角色仍是虚构且由系统编写的，但短信、电话、共度时光与视听互动将角色塑造分布在不同时间和场景中。相对于主线，它们可能是外围内容；但对于玩家感知到的角色完整性，它们同样具有构成作用。知道角色是虚构的，也可以与真实的安慰、反思和依恋并存。

研究边界

问卷样本为 109 位自愿参与者，且偏向长期、投入度较高的玩家；横截面数据能够说明变量之间的关联，不能证明因果。问卷语言渠道不构成正式的跨文化比较；部分构念使用单题项测量，开放回答由单人研究者编码。本研究的结论应结合这些范围理解；后续可通过更大规模的纵向研究、经过验证的多题项量表、正式跨文化比较和跨游戏研究继续检验这一框架。
